# Creative brief: Keelstone Q2 launch

*Sample document. Keelstone is a fictional product created to demonstrate GTM Simulator.*

## The ask

Launch messaging for the Q2 release: website hero, paid social, and a broker-facing one-pager.
We want three distinct directions to test before we commit.

## Audience

Mid-market employers in high-injury industries and the brokers who advise them. Primary reader for
the website is the CFO or the safety manager. Brokers get their own one-pager.

## What we want them to think

"This prevents injuries, and the savings will follow at renewal."

## Single-minded proposition

Fewer injuries, fewer claims, and a better story at renewal, on the cameras you already have.

## Tone

Plain, practical and specific. Talk like a safety professional, not a tech company. Never
fear-based, never shock imagery.

## Mandatories

- Never imply surveillance or discipline. Cameras help crews, they don't watch them.
- Position the broker as a partner, never as someone we go around.
- Only use claims we can prove. Check the MRD proof table.
- Mention "no new hardware" or "the cameras you already have" in at least one direction.

## Directions to explore

1. Cost: lead with premium and total cost of risk.
2. Safety: lead with injury prevention.
3. Partnership: lead with the broker and renewal.
