# MRD: Keelstone go-to-market

*Sample document. Keelstone is a fictional product created to demonstrate the Synthetic Market Simulator.*

**Owner:** Product marketing · **Launch:** Q2 2026

## Market

Mid-market employers (50 to 5,000 employees) in construction, manufacturing, warehousing and
logistics, healthcare and senior living, transportation, agriculture and food processing,
hospitality, and retail. These industries account for most workers' comp claim costs in the
segment. Most buy coverage through an independent broker.

## Buying group

- **Safety manager or EHS director.** Champion and daily user. Usually the first to see the value.
- **CFO or controller.** Economic buyer. Owns the insurance line and wants a payback period.
- **IT manager.** Technical veto on cameras, data and integrations.
- **Insurance broker.** Trusted advisor. Can accelerate or quietly kill the deal.
- **Frontline workers.** Not buyers, but adoption fails without them.

## Competitive landscape

- Point solutions for EHS software (incident tracking, OSHA logs) with no prevention.
- Wearable sensor vendors that need new hardware and worker buy-in.
- Carrier loss-control programs: free, infrequent, and generic.

Keelstone's position: prevention on the cameras customers already own, with a data story that
travels to the broker and carrier.

## Pricing

$450 per site per month, annual contract. Volume pricing above 10 sites. Broker partner program
with a referral fee.
